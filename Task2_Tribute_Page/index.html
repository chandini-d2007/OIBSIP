01 / ABOUT

A mind devoted
to possibility.

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Kalam rose from a modest childhood to become one of India's most respected scientific minds. His work at the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) contributed to the country's aerospace and defence programmes.

Beyond science and public service, he was a passionate teacher who encouraged young people to learn, think boldly and work for a better future. His humility and dedication earned him the affection of millions.

02 / THE JOURNEY

1931

A beginning in Rameswaram

Born on 15 October in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1960

Early scientific career

Joined India's defence research establishment after studying aerospace engineering.

1980

India reaches new heights

Played a key role in the SLV-III project that placed the Rohini satellite in near-Earth orbit.

1997

National recognition

Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.

2002

The People's President

Became the 11th President of India and continued to connect with students across the country.

2015

A teacher until the end

Passed away on 27 July while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong.
